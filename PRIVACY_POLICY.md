# Privacy Policy for Libra Music

**Effective Date:** November 25, 2026  
**Developer:** LibraTech  

LibraTech ("we", "our", or "us") built the **Libra Music** application as a free, ad-free music player app. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**Libra Music does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).**  
- We do not require account registration or login.
- All user data—including playback history, favorite tracks, playlists, and audio equalizer settings—is stored locally on your device.

## 2. App Permissions
To function properly as an audio player, Libra Music may request the following permissions on your Android device:
- **Storage / Read Media Audio:** Used strictly to scan and play local audio files stored on your device.
- **Internet & Network Access:** Used to stream audio tracks, fetch album artwork, and load synchronized lyrics.
- **Foreground Service / Wake Lock:** Used to keep music playing smoothly in the background when the screen is off.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
Libra Music is completely **ad-free** and does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or user-tracking analytics.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our application is safe for all ages (Everyone) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
