# Privacy Policy for Libra Music

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026  
**Developer:** Libra Tech Team  
**Contact:** [libraproject26@gmail.com](mailto:libraproject26@gmail.com)

Libra Tech ("we", "our", or "us") developed **Libra Music** as a free, ad-free music streaming and playback application. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using the Service.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Storage
- **Zero Personal Data Collection:** Libra Music does **not** collect, harvest, track, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No Mandatory Login:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use all features of the application.
- **Local-First Storage:** All your preferences, favorite tracks, and listening history are stored **100% locally** on your device using encrypted/secure device storage (`SharedPreferences`). None of this data is ever sent to external cloud databases.

---

## 2. In-App Feedback & Bug Reports
When you submit a report or feature suggestion using the in-app "Lapor Masalah & Masukan" form:
- Your message, selected category, and optional name/email are transmitted directly to the developer team via a secure HTTPS API endpoint powered by EmailJS.
- This information is solely used to troubleshoot bugs and improve the application.
- We never share, sell, or monetize your feedback data or email addresses.

---

## 3. Device Permissions
To provide a seamless music playback and lyrics experience, Libra Music requests the following permissions:
- **Foreground Service & Notification (`android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`):** Allows audio to play uninterrupted in the background while your screen is locked or while multitasking.
- **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used strictly to fetch audio streams, retrieve synchronized lyrics from community databases (LRCLIB), load album covers, and send feedback reports.
- **Wake Lock (`android.permission.WAKE_LOCK`):** Prevents your device's audio decoder from going into sleep mode during active music playback.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
Libra Music uses minimal third-party endpoints strictly necessary for core functionality:
- **Audio & Search Stream:** Fetched via privacy-friendly, decentralized Invidious / streaming instances without user tracking.
- **Synchronized Lyrics:** Powered by open community lyric databases ([LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net)).
- **Advertisements:** Libra Music is **100% Ad-Free**. There are no third-party ad networks, advertising trackers, or telemetry SDKs embedded in the app.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
Libra Music does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. The app is rated for General Audiences (Everyone).
